The Process

Five stages. No shortcuts.

This is what separates a real restoration from a wipe-on product that fades in a few months.

  1. 01

    Inspection

    Lens condition, depth of oxidation and any cracking are assessed before work begins.

  2. 02

    Professional Sanding

    Oxidation is physically removed with sanding — never masked with a wipe-on liquid.

  3. 03

    Machine Polishing

    Compound and polish stages bring the plastic back to true optical clarity.

  4. 04

    UV Protection

    A protective sealant locks in the finish and slows future oxidation.

  5. 05

    Final Inspection

    Headlights are assessed for optimal clarity. The project area is cleaned, and photos are taken for later delivery to the customer.
